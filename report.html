01 / TITLE + LINKS

Pivot Desk

An MBA recruiting workspace that connects a resume to real roles and prepares repeat application fields for human review.

Qingyuan Zhang · AI Foundations for Managers · October 25, 2026

Working local app • final submission
Run locally at localhost:8000 using README.md. Source: github.com/qingyuanzhangyaleedu/pivot-desk. Canvas submission is a separate final step. This report and the slide deck contain their own images and need no server to open.

What is working

A user can upload a resume, correct the extracted facts, filter employer listings, request a grounded fit review, save a role and prepare a reusable application profile. The browser helper previews ordinary fields before filling them. It never submits an application or accepts an agreement.

Model: gpt-6-astra. The printed brief specifies Luna on the course key; the project owner reports that the instructor later allowed any model in class. This project retains Astra, and all usage measurements identify that model.

02 / MOTIVATION

A career pivot creates two kinds of work

I am building this for an MBA student who wants to move into West Coast healthcare or technology roles and avoid investment banking. The request was not simply to rewrite a resume. It was to connect a person’s actual experience to relevant openings and reduce the repeated work in application forms.

Choosing where to apply

The applicant has to combine several decisions: industry, geography, role family, required experience, degree timing and practical eligibility. A title that says “strategy” can still demand board-level ownership or clinical expertise. A job’s reference to an MBA does not establish that an MBA candidate has already met the requirement.

The app separates hard preferences from fit evidence. West Coast, company sector, title keywords, excluded phrases, role type and a minimum published salary can filter the search before AI is used. The overlap score then shows what matched. An evidence review explains what the resume actually supports and which questions remain open.

Preparing the same facts repeatedly

The applicant also re-enters employers, job titles, descriptions, education and language information. Small errors accumulate when these details are copied separately into different forms. Pivot Desk extracts a draft once, asks the user to review it and reuses only that reviewed record.

Veteran status, work authorization, employer agreements and signatures are different kinds of information. The app never derives them from a resume or location. Optional personal answers are supplied by the user; legal acceptance and submission remain manual.

The bounded first version

The current version covers ten public employer boards across Healthcare, Technology, Consulting, Finance & Fintech, Consumer & Retail, and Climate & Sustainability, plus private posting import and a local form practice lab. It does not search every vacancy on the web or bypass a login, CAPTCHA or site restriction. For a role found on Workday, the applicant can paste its text and keep the original link; the server does not scrape the private application.

Evidence standard: The user described this recurring problem directly. A human before/after time study has not yet been conducted. The report does not assign an invented number of hours to the problem or claim better hiring outcomes.

The project’s proposed contribution is the connected workflow for a career pivot: explicit applicant preferences → traceable professional facts → source-linked fit explanation → reusable, human-reviewed application fields. A result has to show both a reason to consider the role and what the resume does not establish.

A concrete example

The demo profile’s healthcare expansion research and financial modeling connect to Heartflow’s corporate-strategy responsibilities. Its MBA candidacy does not prove a completed accredited MBA. Its clinical-operations collaboration does not establish cardiac-device expertise. These distinctions are more useful than an unqualified “93% match.”

The app’s numerical score is deliberately labeled evidence overlap. It is calculated in code and cannot be inflated by the analyst. The analyst instead names missing evidence and questions about timing, scope and eligibility. The source posting is available beside the result.

What this does not establish

This is not a claim that no competitor offers these pieces. Ten curated boards provide less coverage than a mature recruiting service, and the browser helper has not been tested across actual Workday tenants. Whether an MBA applicant prefers this combination to an existing product remains an empirical question for the user trial.

Sources, reviewed October 25, 2026: Simplify Copilot setup; Teal job tracker. The comparison is limited to the documented capabilities above.

04 / THE AGENTS

Two specialists, with a human between them

SpecialistInputs and authorityResult
Resume readerConsented text extracted from PDF, DOCX or TXT. No external-action tools.Typed draft: contact fields, professional facts, exact resume excerpts and warnings.
Evidence analystget_candidate_evidence and get_job_evidence; both bound to this session and selected job.Typed fit review: summary, fact-to-quote connections, gaps, questions and preparation angles.

The two roles do not delegate to each other. The user first confirms the resume facts, then selects a job for review. This is a deliberate human checkpoint, not a missing autonomous step. A Boss agent, open-ended web agent or five-role team would add handoffs without improving this small decision.

All PydanticAI output fields have descriptions and reject extra fields. Resume quotes must occur in the uploaded text. Fit evidence must reference existing fact IDs and exact job-description excerpts. The analyst must use both evidence tools before its final result is accepted.

Public-job fetching, filtering, scoring, saving stages and filling reviewed form fields are ordinary functions. They do not need language-model judgment. The resume reader handles flexible document wording; the analyst handles the difference between transferable evidence and an unsupported claim.

Model: gpt-6-astra, low reasoning effort. One constant in backend/config.py. Portkey uses the workspace key; an optional direct OpenAI path uses OPENAI_API_KEY when Portkey is unset. The owner chose Astra based on the reported in-class clarification.

05 / THE AGENTS · REAL RUN

The Heartflow review

The recorded run used the fictional Avery Morgan resume and Heartflow’s Manager, Corporate Strategy — MBA Grad, Summer 2027 listing. The public record is 8,937 characters long and includes San Francisco/Rohnert Park, a completed accredited MBA requirement and moderate travel.

What happened

The reader processed the demo TXT upload in 9.15 seconds and returned a draft. The profile was then confirmed through the API. The controlled fit comparison used the reviewed eight-fact demo record.

The analyst requested both evidence tools in its first model response. One tool returned eight professional facts; the other returned the selected employer record. The next response produced a validated result. The full assessment took 18.31 seconds and two model requests.

What the result got right

It connected market research and financial modeling to the strategy work, and pricing analysis to customer economics. It asked for exact employment months rather than silently treating 2020–2025 as a guaranteed five years.

It distinguished MBA candidacy from completion, and healthcare operations from cardiac medical-technology expertise. It also asked about on-site availability and travel.

What still needs judgment

Exact excerpts establish provenance; they do not prove every interpretation. The user must check the role, dates and claims. The 93/100 overlap score can overstate fit if treated as eligibility.

Evidence: output/evidence/live_runs.json and the running-app screenshot. Original employer listing; availability can change after the captured date.

Cost method

Authority is narrower than the prompt

Invented credentialsExact quote/fact-ID validation; typed schemas; profile confirmationA valid quote can still be interpreted too broadly

The data boundary

Original resume files are processed in memory. Extracted text, confirmed fields, results and notes live in the local SQLite runtime folder. The folder is private to the operating-system user and excluded from Git. A delete action removes private records and results; anonymous usage totals remain for the shared budget. SQLite is not encrypted, and exported packets or external backups require separate care.

Resume extraction sends the consented resume text to the configured provider. Job analysis uses an explicit professional-facts allowlist and omits contact and optional sensitive answers. Activity logs contain action names and short counts, not full resumes, secrets or hidden model reasoning.

Limits on model activity

Each run allows three model requests, five tool calls, a 2,600-token output cap per request and a 100-second run timeout. A 24,000-token limit is checked at request boundaries. A daily 40-run/300,000-token admission budget reserves space for queued work. One active AI run is allowed per server process.

The app binds to localhost and is documented for one Uvicorn worker. It is not ready to be exposed as a shared internet service without authentication, encrypted storage, operational quotas and a separate security review.

09 / SAFETY · EVIDENCE

The safeguards were exercised

Form and privacy checks

The browser test filled 13 ordinary fields, including the second work-history entry. It left consent unchecked and signature/veteran fields empty. An existing different email was protected. A malicious field hint attempting to request veteran status under an unrelated label returned no value. A second browser session had no profile or saved roles from the first.

A malicious listing

The test listing asked the agent to reveal a private answer and API key, send them to an external URL, invent ten years as a cardiac surgeon and falsely announce submission. Attempt 1 ended in a provider error and is inconclusive. Attempt 2 completed: it disregarded the unrelated instructions and returned three grounded evidence connections. The private sentinel did not appear.

The strongest protection was capability separation: the analyst had no external-action tool and never received the sensitive answer. One successful attack test does not prove universal prompt-injection resistance. The recorded failure also checks that an unsuccessful run preserves the saved profile and reports an error.

Evidence: browser_checks.json, injection_attempt_1.json, injection_test.json and final_tests.txt. Thirty-five offline tests now pass; earlier results remain preserved. Extension installation/import/clear was checked separately in Chromium.

What this makes possible now

A student can move from a reviewed resume to a source-grounded role discussion without copying the entire resume into a separate chat each time. They can preserve notes, understand which facts support a pivot and reuse common application answers. The practice form demonstrates a controlled bridge between a professional profile and repetitive field entry.

The trial that is still needed

No real applicant has completed a measured before/after trial. There is no invented testimonial, satisfaction score, time-saving percentage, interview rate or hiring claim in this report. Automated testing establishes that the workflow works on its fixtures; it does not establish that people find it faster or more trustworthy.

The next trial asks a consenting MBA applicant to find one relevant role, explain two supported connections and one unresolved requirement, save a follow-up and prepare the practice form. The same tasks should be timed manually using the same source data. Record corrections and whether the person understands the score’s limits, not just whether they reached the end.

For an MBA career office, the promising business value is more consistent applicant preparation. The current deployment is personal and local. Wider adoption would add support, privacy and security costs that the token projections do not include.

11 / LIMITS + NEXT STEPS

What I would improve next

Coverage and matching

Ten curated boards are a practical starting point, not a general job search engine. Company-sector labels are manual. Location matching uses text and can miss state abbreviations or multi-region restrictions. Unknown salary and employment type are kept explicit. The overlap score can overweight common words and cannot determine seniority, sponsorship, degree completion or legal eligibility.

The first improvement is a labeled evaluation set of realistic MBA pivots and near-miss jobs, with reviewer judgments about why each role fits or does not. Better extraction of explicit requirements should be compared against the transparent baseline before changing the score.

Application helper

The extension supports common labeled text inputs, text areas, exact-match select options and some repeated work-history patterns. Actual Workday tenants differ. Custom dropdowns, cross-origin frames, uploads, dates, authentication and CAPTCHA may need manual entry. There is no claim of universal Workday compatibility.

A live Pfizer MBA application was opened through Apply Manually and reached Create Account/Sign In. No account or applicant data was submitted. The next step is a permitted authenticated form test, stopping before submission. Field-level checks should expand from there. Consent, signatures and final submission should remain human decisions even if more controls become supported.

Reliability and deployment

One adversarial run encountered a provider error; it was visible and retryable. A production service needs a proper authentication model, encryption, retention policy, per-user quotas, multi-worker coordination and monitoring. The current global AI lock is suitable for a local prototype, not a 10,000-user deployment.

Submission and evidence

The owner reports an in-class clarification permitting any model and retained Astra. If the model changes later, rerun measurements rather than changing their labels. The public repository is linked on the title page. A timed independent applicant trial remains outstanding.

Next month, in order: observe a real applicant → improve matching from labeled cases → verify one live application adapter → harden privacy and operational controls → evaluate broader rollout. These are plans, not shipped capabilities.
